    Data source and method
    • Data. County Health Rankings & Roadmaps, 2025 California file (University of Wisconsin Population Health Institute). Each measure comes from its own public source; the data years run from 2019 to 2024. Values are used as published; counties missing a value are left out of that analysis.
    • Each dot is a county average, not a person. Counties under 20,000 residents have less reliable estimates, and every county counts equally.
    • Test. H₀: ρ = 0 vs. Hₐ: ρ ≠ 0, two-tailed. t = r√((n − 2)/(1 − r²)), df = n − 2, exact p-value from the t distribution. Same t and p as testing the slope.
    • Strength (rule of thumb): |r| under 0.10 none, under 0.40 weak, under 0.70 moderate, otherwise strong.
    • Trying many pairs will turn up some significant results by chance alone.
